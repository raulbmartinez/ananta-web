Bertoli

Homogenizers and high-pressure pumps for the process industry, an international benchmark in homogenization technology.

Bertoli is an Interpump Group brand dedicated to the design, planning and construction of high-pressure homogenizers and piston pumps for the food industry. It was founded in 1974 by Vittorio Bertoli and, since 2016, has been part of Interpump Group, the world's largest manufacturer of professional high-pressure piston pumps.

Its machines have a sanitary design: all product-contact parts are designed and manufactured to the strictest international standards for the food and pharmaceutical industries.

Nakakin

Equipment and engineering solutions for industrial processes, expanding the technical capacity ANANTA offers its clients.

Nakakin is a Japanese manufacturer of rotary piston pumps, founded in 1950. It makes the main liquid-contact parts of the pump in its own foundry and controls the whole process: from casting and component manufacturing to assembly, performance testing and shipping.

It holds 3-A and EHEDG (European Hygienic Engineering and Design Group) certification, and every pump undergoes performance testing to ensure safety.

Series

JM / JO Series

Internal mechanical seal (JM) or O-ring (JO). Their simple structure, which makes disassembly and assembly easy, their long service life and high performance make them the most popular models.

JMU Series

External mechanical seal, with a choice of single, quench or tandem. Designed for a wide variety of liquids, it works especially well with corrosive liquids and with fibres.

AMXN Series

Designed for fully aseptic liquid transfer: it completely isolates the liquid from the atmosphere. With sterile water and steam, it is suited to aseptic production lines for long-life products such as dairy and medicines.

SC Series

Designed specifically for easy cleaning and washing: its flat cover and the elimination of protrusions allow effective cleaning with less residual liquid.

Quality and cleaning

  • Proprietary "Nakamura Metal No. 3" alloy: patented stainless steel with lower thermal expansion, allowing a 70 µm clearance between rotor and casing, the smallest in the industry.
  • About 100 inspection tests per pump, with adjustments to one hundredth of a millimetre (10 µm) and a full-load operating test.
  • CIP and SIP cleaning: the CIP JET function cleans the inside of the pump without disassembling it.

Technical data

  • Materials: pump housing and cover in AISI 316 stainless steel (1.4571); double-blade rotors in patented alloy.
  • Mechanical seal: carbon/ceramics, tungsten carbide or silicon carbide.
  • O-rings: Viton or EPDM (other materials on request).
  • Connections: DIN 11851, SMS, aseptic flanges DIN 11864-2, aseptic screwed DIN 11864-1 and Tri-clamp ISO 2852.
  • Installation: horizontal or vertical, reversible flow; suction head up to 9 mWS.
  • Certifications: EHEDG, 3-A and CE.

On Fitting

Components and fittings for industrial installations, integrated into our maintenance and assembly projects.

O.N. Fitting is an expansion-type fitting for light-gauge stainless steel pipes, made by O.N. Industries Ltd. (ONK) of Japan. It is installed without heat or flame, requires no specialized technology and is not a pressed-type fitting.

Its principle: the pipe-expanding rubber is expanded by an axial load under oil pressure; a cap nut and a flange are then fitted to the end of the expanded pipe, and the rubber seal, housed in a triangular chamber, seals the liquid. ONK holds ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certificates and the DVGW type examination certificate.

Features

Easy

Easy to install, without needing much time to acquire the skills. Tightening can be checked visually through the conical washer, so the cap nut is never left loose.

Fast

With a dedicated pipe-expanding machine, anyone can do the work. Prefabrication by cutting and expanding pipes to drawings can be done in a pipe workshop, and final on-site assembly is simpler.

Strong

Excellent durability, with a unique structure and expansion system and proven seismic testing. Its products have been verified to withstand earthquakes of up to approximately MSK XII, applying an acceleration of 2,000 Gal to the pipes.

Light

It uses thin-wall stainless steel pipes: easy to transport, handle and operate even in tight spaces.

Eco-friendly

Hygienic stainless steel does not produce red or blue water from pipe corrosion, is environmentally friendly and has a long service life. As no open flame is used, it is ideal for repairs.

Tote Bin Dumper

Unloading machine designed and built by ANANTA to automatically empty tote bins holding aseptic bags of raw material, with virtually no product loss and just one operator per shift.

The bin arrives on roller conveyors, is lifted and turned over to extract the aseptic bag. The bag is washed with pressurized water, cut by rotary blades, and a roller press empties all the product into a hopper, ready to be sent to the process line.

The whole cycle is PLC-controlled and can be integrated with the plant control system.

How it works

Dumping

A cylinder lifts and turns the bin over, held by upper locks and lateral barriers, to extract the aseptic bag.

Washing

14 diffusers wash the bag with water at 22 psi; a plastic curtain removes the water and a roller presses the product to the bottom.

Cutting and emptying

Plain-edge rotary blades in AISI 316L cut the bag and a roller press empties the product into the hopper.

Control and CIP

PLC with touch panel, SCADA connectivity and a CIP system that cleans every product-contact part with pressurized water.

Construction

  • AISI 304 stainless steel; cutting blades and product hopper in AISI 316 with mirror finish.
  • U-shaped roller conveyors with a buffer of 3 full and 3 empty bins, expandable to 5, plus forklift protection.
  • SIL3 safety system per EN 62061 and selective overcurrent protection.
  • Optional remote control module for supervision from SCADA or historian servers.

Compared with suction unloading

  • 1 person per shift, free to handle other tasks while the machine runs, instead of 2 to 4 fully dedicated.
  • Product losses of ≈0.5%, versus the usual 3–20%.
  • Very short payback period thanks to savings in product and labour.
