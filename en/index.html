What we do

Comprehensive industrial maintenance, from diagnosis to start-up

We are an industrial maintenance company specialised in the reliability of production assets. We repair pumps, compressors, gearboxes and other rotating equipment, apply predictive maintenance and carry out installations and plant shutdowns with our own teams.

Industrial maintenance

Rotating equipment repair, maintenance engineering and condition-based maintenance to reduce unplanned downtime.

  • Pump and rotating equipment repair
  • Vibration analysis and thermography
  • Valve overhaul and surface repair
