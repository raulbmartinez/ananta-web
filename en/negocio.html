Business

ANANTA takes part in every phase of the facility life cycle, providing comprehensive solutions that ensure efficiency, reliability and operational continuity.

From the project phase through construction and assembly of the facility, commissioning, maintenance and management, we support our clients at every stage of the project. Our multidisciplinary experience and the commitment of our teams allow us to offer a global, flexible service adapted to the needs of each sector, contributing to the success and sustainability of the facilities over time.

Industrial Maintenance

We manage your maintenance, from small repairs to the implementation of large turnkey projects, across all types of industry sectors.

Dynamic equipment repair

Our activity brings together all the essential components for improving the reliability of production assets and, unlike other companies, we have in-depth experience and knowledge of both the machines and the different production and repair processes, which allows us to successfully and reliably take on the repair of equipment.

At our facilities we offer solutions for correcting problems. We study the possible improvements to apply, always using the most cutting-edge technology, and, in agreement with the client, we draw up the most suitable work procedure.

Maintenance engineering

Design of maintenance plans tailored to the criticality of each asset, with risk analysis and reliability criteria, for critical equipment, production lines and automation systems.

Condition Based Maintenance (CBM)

Analysis of vibration, temperature, pressure or lubrication to detect early signs of failure and plan the intervention precisely, reducing unplanned downtime and maintenance costs.

Integral maintenance

The most complete solution to guarantee the operation of equipment, facilities and production systems: preventive, corrective and technical supervision actions, with plans tailored to the real needs of each plant.

Surface repair engineering

We restore parts damaged by wear, corrosion or abrasion, extending their service life.

  • Surface treatment against chemical attack
  • Surface treatment against mechanical abrasion
  • Surface reconstruction
  • Structural reinforcements
  • Tuning of dynamic equipment
  • Installation and assembly

Valve inspection

We have qualified personnel, specialized equipment and our own logistics support for the inspection, repair, calibration and testing of all types of valves, ensuring their performance and operational safety.

Component manufacturing

We design, manufacture and adapt custom parts and spares to the specific needs of each machine and production process: the right part, with the fit and material the job requires, at the lowest total cost.

  • Precision: machining and restoration of tolerances, with materials and finishes suited to each use, such as stainless-steel rotor blades and paddles.
  • Economy: we replace only what is needed, without changing complete assemblies.
  • Less downtime: we cut waiting times and reliance on external suppliers.
  • Workshop equipment: lathes, mills, CNC machines and blanchers.

Assembly & Shutdowns

Full execution of industrial assembly projects and plant shutdowns, start to finish.

Industrial assembly projects

Planning, scheduling and execution of industrial assembly, from design to commissioning, with the same technical rigour on a small intervention as on a turnkey project.

  • Scope: installation and assembly of equipment, process lines and production systems.
  • Planning: each phase scheduled to meet deadlines and avoid rework.
  • Continuity: the same team supports the project through to commissioning.

Industrial machinery relocation

Turnkey dismantling, transport and reinstallation of industrial machinery, minimizing downtime and preserving the precision of each machine.

  • Dismantling: prior analysis and orderly dismantling of each machine.
  • Transport: safe transfer of the machinery to its new location.
  • Reinstallation: assembly, alignment and commissioning at destination.

Start-up and shutdowns

Planning and execution of scheduled shutdowns and the subsequent start-up, minimizing the impact on production.

  • Before: planning of the work and of the resources needed for the shutdown.
  • During: maintenance, repairs and assembly carried out within the available window.
  • After: start-up and testing to return the plant to production.

Alignment & balancing of dynamic equipment

Laser alignment and dynamic balancing of pumps, turbines and rotating equipment, in the workshop or on site.

  • Dynamic balancing: rotors from 100 mm to 3890 mm in diameter and from 40 to 95,000 rpm.
  • Alignment: complete drivetrains with coupling or belt transmissions.
  • Result: lower vibration and wear, and longer equipment service life.

Automation & Control

Beyond PLC programming: we design and start up PLC-based control systems, adapting ourselves to the client's needs and integrating them with other existing systems in the plant.

Our philosophy is to maximize what technology can provide to improve our clients' industrial processes.

Automation and control of processes

Our automation services start with understanding the client's needs, to fulfil them in a project that gathers what is to be implemented: from modifying an existing installation to the design and set-up of a Distributed Control System (DCS). We have a team of engineers who provide automation solutions to improve or install control systems in an effective and safe manner.

Our automation services include:

  • Design and manufacturing of MCC control panels
  • HMI and PLC programming
  • Implementation of Distributed Control Systems (DCS)
  • Start-up and testing
  • Client training to operate the system
  • Maintenance and technical assistance

Industrial Control Systems (ICS)

Supervision and control of industries in a centralized manner and in real time.

The incorporation of industrial control systems in the plant may aid more efficient production and maintain a high quality standard for all products. Industrial Control Systems (ICS) are essential to accomplish cost, quantity and quality objectives.

Our services include:

  • Design of communication systems
  • Installation of remote supervision and control systems
  • SCADA system implementation
  • Measures to protect ICS
  • Implementation of industrial security systems

ICS integration with IT systems and Business Intelligence (BI)

Manage and optimize the industrial process: turn field data into useful information for your business.

The way to extract all the value from an automation investment is to interconnect the data obtained in industrial operations with corporate applications. The objective is to deliver all the relevant data managed in the industrial process control room. This way we manage to take decisions based on real and updated data, minimizing risk and increasing value.

Our services include:

  • Control room management
  • Development of SCADA applications
  • Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES): orientation and management of manufacturing processes
  • Integration of MES systems with Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems
  • Integration of databases in real time with corporate applications or systems
  • Business Intelligence solutions to set in context, exploit and disseminate information
  • Cloud Computing
  • Industrial security systems: minimize transaction risk between industrial and corporate areas
